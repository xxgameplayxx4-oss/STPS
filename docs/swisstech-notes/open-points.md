## AGB: legal substance a lawyer should look at (language polished, meaning unchanged)
1. 1.2 vs 3.2/10.1: STPS "offers manufacture" but elsewhere only "coordinates" it. Contract manufacturer or intermediary? Affects warranty and liability.
2. 15.2 vs 15.5: gross negligence "unaffected", yet the liability cap covers "any liability". 15.1 and 15.2 overlap.
3. 20.4: "trägt die Verantwortung für Ansprüche Dritter" does not say whether it is an indemnity.
4. 20.2: "für andere Zwecke" does not say other than what.
5. 23.5/23.7: no payment deadline anywhere; default and 5% interest have no fixed start date.
6. 13.1 vs 6.5/14.2: 12-month warranty runs from "delivery", 14.1 ties it to transfer of risk; can diverge under non-EXW Incoterms.
7. 5.4 and 22.4: "behält sich vor … zu vereinbaren" is only a right to negotiate, not to adjust.
8. 24.1/24.2: retention of title only "where agreed"; in Switzerland it needs a register entry to be effective.
9. 21.3: non-circumvention duty has no term and no consequence.
10. 4.3 and 22.2: overlapping duties to inform.
11. 29.4: check the venue (court for Freienbach is in Höfe/Pfäffikon SZ).

- Einzelfirma (2026-10-15): Under Swiss law (Art. 945 OR) an Einzelfirma's registered name must contain the owner's family name (e.g. "SwissTech Precision Solutions Walser"). The client must confirm the exact registered name; the site currently uses "SwissTech Precision Solutions" with no suffix.
